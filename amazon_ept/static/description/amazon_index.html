1. How can I create a support ticket for assistance with my purchased connector? +
You can raise a support ticket by following the steps below:
a. Log in or sign up on our website: https://www.emiprotechnologies.com/web/login
b. Go to "My Account" and click on "Register My Order".
c. Enter your Sale Order Number and save the details.
d. After successful registration, navigate to the "Helpdesk" menu and create a new support ticket.
Our support team will review your request and get back to you accordingly.
2. Does purchasing the app from the Odoo App Store include all features? +
With the purchase of the Emipro Amazon Connector from the Odoo app store, you get access to all the published features of the connector along with the connector IAP services.
3. Is there a recurring fee after purchasing the app? +
Yes. This connector is available as an In-App Purchase (IAP) module. After the complimentary IAP period expires, continued usage of the connector requires purchasing recurring IAP packs, which are available on a monthly or annual basis.
4. Is the app compatible with both Odoo Enterprise and Community editions? +
Yes. The app is compatible with Odoo Enterprise (On-Premise and Odoo.sh) as well as Odoo Community. However, Odoo Online (Cloud) does not allow third-party app installations, so the connector cannot be used on Odoo Online.
5. How can I request additional features? +
Additional features can be developed as part of a customization request. You can contact us by creating a support ticket to discuss your requirements (website : https://www.emiprotechnologies.com/web/login).
6. Are upgrades and support included with the purchase? +
Each Odoo version of the connector is treated as a separate product version. Connectors are not automatically upgraded to newer Odoo versions, and a version-specific connector must be purchased separately.
7. Does the connector support multiple Amazon Seller accounts? +
Yes, the Amazon Odoo Connector supports integration with multiple Seller accounts.
8. Which Amazon marketplaces are supported? +
The connector supports the following Amazon marketplaces : US, Canada, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Egypt, Netherlands, Poland, Saudi Arabia, Sweden, Singapore, Australia, Mexico, Japan, UAE, Turkey, and Brazil.
9. Are additional server libraries required to use the connector? +
No additional libraries are required beyond the standard dependency module (common connector library).
10. Does the connector support Pan-European (PAN-EU) FBA? +

11. Does the connector support the OSS (One-Stop Shop) program for Europe? +
Yes, the connector supports the OSS program for the European region.
12. Can past Amazon orders be imported into Odoo? +
Both historical FBM and FBA orders can be imported into Odoo.
13. Can I integrate an Amazon Vendor Central account using this connector? +
No. Vendor Central accounts cannot be integrated using Amazon Connector because Amazon Vendor Central and Seller Central operate under different platforms and business models. This connector supports Seller Central only.
14. Are settlement reports supported in Odoo Community? +
No, settlement report functionality is not available in the Odoo Community version.
15. Can I export new products from Odoo to Amazon using this connector? +
No. The connector does not support exporting new products from Odoo to Amazon. Instead, it provides product import and product mapping features to synchronize products between both platforms.
16. Why does customer information get deleted from my Amazon sales orders in Odoo after 30 days? +
In compliance with Amazon Data Protection Policy, customer data fetched into Odoo from Amazon is automatically removed after 30 days.
The following information is deleted from the sales order :
a. Name
b. Email ID
c. Phone Number
d. Billing and Shipping Address
If you are legally required to retain personally identifiable information (PII) for tax or regulatory purposes, such data must be stored in a "cold" or offline backup. This backup must be encrypted and kept in a physically secure location, with no immediate or interactive access. These guidelines are outlined in the Amazon Seller Registration page Terms and Conditions. Please ensure you review them during the seller registration process.
