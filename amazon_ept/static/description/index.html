The Amazon Connector for Odoo centralizes your Amazon operations inside a single ERP system, covering both Amazon FBA and FBM workflows end to end. From product synchronization and order processing to FBA inventory, settlement reconciliation, returns, and reimbursements, the connector ensures operational accuracy and financial integrity at scale. It supports multiple seller accounts and marketplaces, advanced FBA shipment handling, automated schedulers, and deep accounting reconciliation using Amazon settlement reports.

Built for high-volume sellers, the connector eliminates manual intervention, reduces reconciliation errors, and provides full visibility across sales, inventory, and accounting - all from Odoo.

Highlights

Order Processing & Fulfillment

Unify all your Amazon Orders (FBM + FBA) from various marketplaces in Odoo, process them & fulfill them with ease. Send shipment tracking information to your Amazon customers right from Odoo

Stock Synchronization

Say good-bye to your worries about overselling or underselling. Automatically synchronize your stock levels between Amazon & Odoo and ensure that all your Amazon marketplaces show the accurate & updated stock levels

Catalog Management

Synchronize your products & variations between Odoo & Amazon and effectively manage product pricing, and a lot more using the CSV and Excel files. Effortlessly map your Amazon Products with Odoo

Settlement Report Processing

Bring all your expenses, commissions & payments from Amazon to Odoo by downloading the Settlement Report in Odoo and processing & reconciling it to match up with your accounting

Order Cancellation

Improve your customer service by keeping an accurate track of order cancellations in Amazon. Fetch all order cancellations from Amazon to Odoo and also perform cancellations from Odoo to Amazon.

Invoicing & Refunds

Forget the accounting hassles and manage the invoicing completely in Odoo for your Amazon Orders. Upload the invoices to Amazon directly from Odoo. Keep track of refund requests & process refunds of your Amazon Orders from Odoo.

Inbound Shipments

Hassle free inbound shipments right from Odoo! Create a shipment plan, add products to the shipment and send it to Amazon. If you prefer to create inbound shipments on Amazon, fetch them to Odoo using the Shipment ID

FBA Stock Adjustment

Amazon generates an Inventory Adjustment Report while making changes to the stock levels of your FBA Inventory. Automatically, fetch the report to Odoo and accordingly ensure stock adjustments to your Odoo warehouse.

Removal Order Processing

Want to remove your inventory from FBA warehouses? Create a removal order in Odoo or Amazon, specify if you want to return it to a particular warehouse or dispose it and have the removal order processed in Odoo

Multi-Channel Fulfillment

Managing multiple sales channels using Amazon's MCF services? Don't worry, we got you covered. Prepare & submit the outbound order information from Odoo directly to Amazon's fulfillment centers

Seamless Automation

Don't worry about remembering to perform various routine Amazon operations by enabling automation features. Automate order import, stock synchronization, price update, and much more

Multiple Seller Accounts

Expand your international presence by connecting multiple seller accounts to your Odoo Instance and multiple marketplaces per seller account

Seller Rating Report

Now review your sellers' feedback by importing seller rating report. Order wise, rating wise, & customer wise, report can be viewed and accordingly corrective actions can be taken.

VCS Program

Amazon provides a VAT calculation service if a seller has opted for it, with the help of this connector, they can store an Amazon Invoice number for the Posted/Paid invoices in the Odoo.

Amazon Fulfilment Programs

Based on various fulfilment programs like PAN EU, CFN, EFN, MCI, necessary configurations will be done automatically in Odoo like warehouse creation & Tax configurations.

How can I create a support ticket for assistance with my purchased connector?
You can raise a support ticket by following the steps below:
a.Log in or sign up on our website: https://www.emiprotechnologies.com/web/login
b.Go to "My Account" and click on "Register My Order".
c.Enter your Sale Order Number and save the details.
d.After successful registration, navigate to the "Helpdesk" menu and create a new support ticket.
Our support team will review your request and get back to you accordingly.
Does purchasing the app from the Odoo App Store include all features?
With the purchase of the Emipro Amazon Connector from the Odoo app store, you get access to all the published features of the connector along with the connector IAP services.
Is there a recurring fee after purchasing the app?
Yes. This connector is available as an In-App Purchase (IAP) module. After the complimentary IAP period expires, continued usage of the connector requires purchasing recurring IAP packs, which are available on a monthly or annual basis.
Is the app compatible with both Odoo Enterprise and Community editions?
Yes. The app is compatible with Odoo Enterprise (On-Premise and Odoo.sh) as well as Odoo Community. However, Odoo Online (Cloud) does not allow third-party app installations, so the connector cannot be used on Odoo Online.
How can I request additional features?
Additional features can be developed as part of a customization request. You can contact us by creating a support ticket to discuss your requirements (website : https://www.emiprotechnologies.com/web/login).
Are upgrades and support included with the purchase?
Each Odoo version of the connector is treated as a separate product version. Connectors are not automatically upgraded to newer Odoo versions, and a version-specific connector must be purchased separately.
Does the connector support multiple Amazon Seller accounts?
Yes, the Amazon Odoo Connector supports integration with multiple Seller accounts.
Which Amazon marketplaces are supported?
The connector supports the following Amazon marketplaces : US, Canada, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Egypt, Netherlands, Poland, Saudi Arabia, Sweden, Singapore, Australia, Mexico, Japan, UAE, Turkey, and Brazil.
Are additional server libraries required to use the connector?
No additional libraries are required beyond the standard dependency module (common connector library).
Does the connector support Pan-European (PAN-EU) FBA?
Yes, Pan-European FBA is supported.
Does the connector support the OSS (One-Stop Shop) program for Europe?
Yes, the connector supports the OSS program for the European region.
Can past Amazon orders be imported into Odoo?
Both historical FBM and FBA orders can be imported into Odoo.
Can I integrate an Amazon Vendor Central account using this connector?
No. Vendor Central accounts cannot be integrated using Amazon Connector because Amazon Vendor Central and Seller Central operate under different platforms and business models. This connector supports Seller Central only.
Are settlement reports supported in Odoo Community?
No, settlement report functionality is not available in the Odoo Community version.
Can I export new products from Odoo to Amazon using this connector?
No. The connector does not support exporting new products from Odoo to Amazon. Instead, it provides product import and product mapping features to synchronize products between both platforms.
Why does customer information get deleted from my Amazon sales orders in Odoo after 30 days?
In compliance with Amazon Data Protection Policy, customer data fetched into Odoo from Amazon is automatically removed after 30 days. The following information is deleted from the sales order :
a.Name
b.Email ID
c.Phone Number
d.Billing and Shipping Address
If you are legally required to retain personally identifiable information (PII) for tax or regulatory purposes, such data must be stored in a "cold" or offline backup. This backup must be encrypted and kept in a physically secure location, with no immediate or interactive access. These guidelines are outlined in the Amazon Seller Registration page Terms and Conditions. Please ensure you review them during the seller registration process.
